# Species Mass Proportions

Each species should have **distinct body-part proportions** (tissue and bone max) so they feel different in combat and support different tactics.

---

## Current species

### Human (segment-mass baseline)

- **Proportions:** Chest > leg > head = arm (de Leva / Plagenhoef: trunk ~43%, leg ~20% each, head ~7%, arm ~5% each).
- **Values:** head 10, chest 30, arm 10, leg 26 (each).
- **Feel:** Center mass and legs take the most; limbs and head are equal. Balanced.

### Goblin (top-heavy, spindly limbs)

- **Proportions:** Large head and chest share; thin arms and legs.
- **Values:** head 8, chest 14, arm 4, leg 6 (each).
- **Feel:** Small overall; limbs are very fragile (easy to sever or break). Focusing head/chest is relatively less efficient for their size; limb focus is strong.

---

## Adding a new species

1. Choose a **proportion theme** (e.g. limb-heavy, lean, childlike, brute).
2. Set **tissue max** per part so ratios differ from Human and Goblin.
3. Set **bone max** in line with tissue (e.g. ~0.6–0.8× tissue per part, or match ratios).

**Example ideas:**

| Species | Theme        | Proportions (head/chest/arm/leg) | Notes                          |
|---------|--------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Orc     | Limb-heavy   | 10 / 32 / 14 / 28                 | Big arms/chest; leg focus      |
| Elf     | Lean         | 8 / 24 / 12 / 24                  | Slightly smaller chest/head    |
| Dwarf   | Compact      | 12 / 28 / 12 / 20                 | Shorter legs, stout head/chest |

Keep total tissue in a range that fits intended durability (e.g. human total ~112; goblin ~42; orc ~128).
